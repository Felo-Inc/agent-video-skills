# Meet Meltica

**Never wonder what to post again.**

[Meltica](https://meltica.ai) is an AI promo-video platform for product brands, App & SaaS teams,
and local businesses. It turns a rough idea — or a video you already saw and liked — into a
concrete short-video plan you can review, confirm and publish. The point is not to stare at a
blank timeline: it is to get from "we should post something" to a usable short video, fast.

> [Try Meltica →](https://meltica.ai)

## The problem it solves

Posting consistently is the hard part of short video. Teams know they *should* publish, but the
work piles up: what to make, which hook to open with, how to turn a product or a storefront into
something worth watching. Meltica attacks the planning half of that problem first — so you always
have a next video to make — then helps you carry it through to a generated draft you control.

## Who it is for

- **Product brands** — turn a product and a campaign goal into a short promotional clip.
- **App & SaaS teams** — explain a feature or a launch with a short, focused video.
- **Local businesses** — plan a store event, a new arrival, or a seasonal promo against a real
  calendar instead of improvising day by day.
- **Creators** — start from a reference video you like and build an original short of your own.

## How it works

1. **Start from an idea or a reference.** Describe what you want to promote, or explore trending
   short-video references for structure and inspiration. References guide the shape of a video —
   they are not copied frame by frame.
2. **Get a rolling topic plan.** For brands and businesses, Meltica drafts a three-day rolling set
   of topics from your brand information and the target market's context — new arrivals, holidays,
   launches — so there is always a next post lined up.
3. **Review, confirm, generate.** You decide what to keep before a video is generated, and you stay
   in control of what gets published. On TikTok the workflow is download, then upload it yourself.
4. **Look back and adjust.** Performance views and history help you see what worked and plan the
   next batch around it.

The product speaks **English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese**, with light
and dark themes.

## What is available today

- An AI-assisted creation flow from idea/reference → topic plan → short-video draft.
- Short-video reference discovery with manual, on-demand fetching — no automatic refresh or
  surprise billing.
- A three-day rolling planning view, and a content calendar you can build around real dates.
- Multi-language interface and a guided onboarding that gets you to your first ideas quickly.
- User confirmation at generation and publishing steps; API keys stay server-side.

## Why this skill collection exists

The 23 skills in this repository are the same building blocks Meltica is built around — finding
creative references, writing hooks and structured video prompts, planning shots, generating clips,
lip sync, localization, editing, sound design, subtitles, thumbnails and repurposing long videos
into shorts. We publish them as open, MIT-licensed Agent Skills because good video tooling should
be usable anywhere: in your own agent, with your own models and keys, with or without Meltica.

If you just want the finished, hosted version of that workflow, start at [meltica.ai](https://meltica.ai).
If you want to assemble your own, every skill here is yours to copy.

## From skills to Meltica

This is a map of the problem space, not a claim about Meltica's internal implementation. Every skill
below can be used on its own; Meltica packages the nearby steps into one guided flow from an idea or
a reference to a short-video draft you review.

| Workflow step | Skills in this repo | Where it fits in Meltica |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand a reference | `watch-video`, `video-understanding` | Start from a video you like and learn its structure instead of facing a blank timeline. |
| Decide what to say | `ugc-ad-scripts`, `product-video-hooks` | Shape the hook and the short-video plan before anything is generated. |
| Write the plan | `video-prompt-studio`, `seedance-video`, `remotion-video`, `motion-graphics-video`, `remotion-best-practices` | Turn the idea into structured prompts, shots and a plan you can review. |
| Carry it end to end | `ai-video-production` | Follow one narrated explainer or music-video-style idea through a complete production flow. |
| Generate the draft | `openrouter-video-gen`, `text-to-video`, `ai-avatar`, `lipsync` | Generate a draft from the confirmed plan, with a person on screen when needed. |
| Adapt for markets | `video-localization` | Carry one master across languages with dubbing and market-specific captions. |
| Finish and repurpose | `ffmpeg-video-edit`, `ai-video-editor`, `video-enhance`, `sound-design`, `video-subtitle`, `thumbnail-video`, `video-to-shorts`, `video-clip-extractor` | Refine the cut, add sound and captions, then repurpose it into shorts or highlight clips. |

## Honest scope

Meltica is an AI-assisted creation tool, not a one-click finished-ad machine. Supplying your own
product photos or footage to have them rendered directly into a finished product commercial is
**not** an available capability today. Sample clips shown on the site are demonstrations of what
the workflow can produce, not proof of an automatic product-footage renderer. Pricing and plan
details on the site are indicative until finalized. Publishing always goes through a step you
confirm, and availability of platform integrations depends on the account you connect.
