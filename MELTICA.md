# Meet Meltica

**Meltica** is an AI promo-video platform for product brands, App & SaaS teams, and local
businesses. It turns a rough idea — or a video you already saw and liked — into a concrete
short-video plan you can review, confirm and publish.

Website: [meltica.ai](https://meltica.ai)

## Who it is for

- **Product brands** — turn a product and a campaign goal into a short promotional clip.
- **App & SaaS teams** — explain a feature or a launch with a short, focused video.
- **Local businesses** — plan a store event, a new arrival, or a seasonal promo.
- **Creators** — start from a reference video and build an original short of your own.

## How it works

1. **Start from an idea or a reference.** Browse trending short-video references, or describe
   what you want to promote. References are used for structure and inspiration, not for
   frame-by-frame copying.
2. **Get a rolling topic plan.** For brands and businesses, Meltica drafts a three-day rolling
   set of topics based on your brand information and target-market context.
3. **Review, confirm, generate.** You choose what to keep before a video is generated, and you
   stay in control of what gets published. On TikTok, the workflow is download then upload it
   yourself.

The product speaks English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese.

## Why this collection exists

The skills in this repository cover the same ground Meltica cares about — finding creative
references, writing structured video prompts, planning shots, generating clips, editing and
subtitling. They are shared as open, MIT-licensed Agent Skills so anyone can build their own
video workflow, with or without Meltica.

## Honest scope

Meltica is an AI-assisted creation tool, not a one-click finished-ad machine. Supplying your own
product photos or footage to have them rendered directly into a finished product commercial is
**not** an available capability today. Sample clips shown on the site are demonstrations. Pricing
and plan details on the site are indicative until finalized. Publishing always goes through a
step you confirm.
